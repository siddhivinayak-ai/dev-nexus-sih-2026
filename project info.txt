. Same Contractor, 3 Accounts: A single contractor registers multiple accounts/entities to submit

. Subcontractor Exploitation: Winning contractors pass off money or commissions to ring members
under the guise of fake subcontracting.

. Regional Market Allocation: Contractors intentionally abstain from bidding in each other's
geographical regions to split the market.

. Unchecked Overpriced Cover Bids: The government fails to verify if losing bids (e.g., from bidders

. Bypassing Portal Checks via Different Bank Accounts/VPNs: Contractors use separate bank
branches, accounts, and VPNs/IP addresses to convince legacy portals that they are distinct bidders.
. Copied Technical Proposals: Bidders B & C submit tenders copied directly from bidder A, which
human reviewers routinely miss during manual evaluations.

. Discrepancy in Operational Capacity: Contractors claim to supply massive volumes (e.g., supply
500 tons) without having the necessary logistics, transport, or storage capabilities.
